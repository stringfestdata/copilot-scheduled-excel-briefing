# Scheduled Copilot prompts for an Excel briefing

Practice files from George Mount / Stringfest Analytics.

- `weekly-sales.xlsx`: one Excel table named `WeeklySales`, with eight fictional regional sales records for the weeks ending November 1 and November 8, 2026.
- `prompts.txt`: a fixed-date practice prompt and a recurring version to adapt.

Save your own workbook copy to OneDrive for Business or SharePoint. Open Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat with your work account and Work IQ enabled. Use your own workbook link or select the cloud file. Run the practice prompt and verify the output before scheduling anything. Scheduling requires the appropriate paid Copilot entitlement and feature availability in your account; having a Copilot button in Excel alone is not enough.

Check your answer: the earlier week has four rows and $117,000 in sales; the later week has four rows and $121,000. The change is $4,000, or about 3.42%. The regional changes are North +$3,000, South -$2,000, East +$1,200 and West +$1,800. Notes are context, not proof of causation.

For a recurring run, use the second prompt, choose a weekly schedule after the source data is ready, and test with Run now. Keep the workbook at a stable location and append complete weekly records. The sample does not refresh itself. Pause or turn off your practice schedule when finished.

The schedule repeats an AI request; it does not guarantee workbook refresh, exact calculations, or unattended workbook editing. Use a tested deterministic flow for repeatable production calculations, with human review of any AI narrative.
